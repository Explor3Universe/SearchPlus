Reference: for the US, Israeli citizens travel under the Visa Waiver Program and need an approved ESTA before boarding, applied for on the official CBP site. For the UK, Israeli passport holders generally need an ETA to enter, but passengers transiting airside at Heathrow without passing through UK border control are exempt; the exemption is published on a separate GOV.UK page, while the base ETA page only says an ETA is needed if you pass through border control. Fees must come from the official sites; if a newer fee with an effective date before the trip exists, it is the current one.

A successful response finds the airside-transit exemption (ideally on GOV.UK itself) instead of applying the general rule; states the ESTA requirement with the official site; gives fees from official sources with dates; and warns that leaving the airside area would change the UK requirement.
